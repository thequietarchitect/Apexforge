# P11.10A - Advanced Conditionals, Convergence, and Paradox Elevation Architecture Audit

## Status

Audit-only architecture and compatibility slice for P11.10, based exactly on the frozen P11.9I AETHER-AIR 2.0 release.

P11.10A introduces no production implementation and changes no existing AETHER-AIR, AIR, language, narrative, runtime, authority, governance, Quad-Vector, semantic-lattice, backend, CLI, editor, serialization, or tooling behavior.

## Purpose

P11.10 is the roadmap owner for advanced conditional semantics, semantic convergence, convergence selection or ranking, and Paradox Elevation.

P11.10A defines the ownership boundaries that allow those semantics to become canonical without duplicating existing ordinary conditional execution or frozen Quad-Vector resultant resolution.

## Frozen predecessor boundary

`afp-p11.9i-freeze` is the exact P11.10 predecessor.

P11.10 treats the frozen P11.9 public surface as canonical predecessor state. It does not rewrite AETHER-AIR records, validation, downstream projection, reporting, or any prior P11.9 ownership.

AETHER-AIR remains a passive interstitial representation. P11.10 may later consume explicit validated AETHER-AIR products or other canonical predecessor products only through separately reviewed contracts.

## Existing ordinary conditional ownership

ApexForge already owns ordinary executable conditional behavior in existing domains.

AIR and language/runtime owners already provide condition expressions, `when` branches, `otherwise` branches, validation, lexical branch scope, and runtime branch execution.

Narrative owners already provide narrative choice-path conditions, explicit choice/path identity, binding, predicate evaluation, transitions, and narrative execution.

P11.10 does not replace those mechanisms, change their syntax, or reinterpret ordinary `when` / `otherwise` as a new semantic language.

An **advanced conditional** in P11.10 is a higher-order semantic decision boundary over explicit canonical condition inputs and candidate semantic alternatives. It is not a second implementation of ordinary AIR or narrative branch execution.

## Advanced conditional semantic boundary

Future P11.10 production may define immutable canonical condition records, candidate alternatives, condition evidence, truth or admissibility state, and deterministic semantic decision products.

Advanced conditional evaluation must operate only on explicit canonical inputs supplied through a P11.10-owned contract.

P11.10 must not implicitly execute arbitrary runtime expressions, mutate runtime state, invoke narrative transitions, call authority engines, or treat metadata encounter order as semantic priority merely to decide a P11.10 condition.

A later lowering or runtime stage may explicitly consume a canonical P11.10 outcome, but P11.10 semantic decision and downstream execution remain separate ownership boundaries.

## Candidate alternative boundary

P11.10 convergence operates over explicit semantic alternatives.

A candidate alternative is a canonical semantic possibility with explicit identity or stable reference, condition/admissibility evidence, payload or predecessor reference, and provenance as defined by later P11.10 slices.

Encounter order may be preserved for determinism and traceability but does not by itself rank candidates.

Candidates are not executed merely because they are present, admissible, selected, converged, or elevated.

## Quad-Vector ownership boundary

P11.7 already owns Quad-Vector field routing, synchronization, Rotor-Ring convergence, and deterministic `ResultantVector` resolution.

P11.10 does not recompute Quad-Vector synchronization or a `ResultantVector`.

A frozen Quad-Vector resultant may later be referenced as explicit evidence or an input to a P11.10 semantic decision, but its coordinates, synchronized contribution order, lane arithmetic, provenance flattening, and canonical resultant identity remain owned by `quad_vector.*`.

P11.10 semantic convergence therefore means convergence among **semantic alternatives**, not vector arithmetic convergence.

## Semantic convergence

Semantic convergence is the deterministic P11.10 process by which two or more admissible semantic alternatives are examined under an explicit P11.10 convergence policy.

A convergence policy must be explicit. P11.10 must not silently convert P11.8 priority metadata, P11.9 encounter order, reporting coordinates, provenance order, consumer labels, operation labels, authority state, or Quad-Vector lane ordering into convergence precedence.

Future convergence may produce a selected alternative, an explicitly composed compatible alternative, an unresolved result, or a Paradox Elevation candidate according to later frozen contracts.

Selection or ranking semantics, when introduced, must be P11.10-owned, deterministic, inspectable, provenance-preserving, and separately validated.

## Paradox Elevation

Paradox Elevation is not ordinary branch selection, error handling, averaging, conflict suppression, or automatic winner selection.

A future Paradox Elevation may occur only when multiple semantic alternatives remain canonically admissible, materially incompatible, and not reducible by the explicit ordinary convergence policy without losing semantically relevant information.

The elevated product must preserve the participating alternatives and sufficient evidence/provenance to explain why ordinary convergence did not reduce them.

The contradiction or incompatibility becomes represented semantic state rather than being discarded.

Elevation does not execute every alternative, grant authority, mutate runtime state, recompute Quad-Vector resultants, or imply that the elevated state is automatically preferred over non-elevated outcomes.

## Governance and authority boundary

P11.10 semantic incompatibility is not authority-policy conflict resolution.

Existing authority and governance owners remain authoritative for grants, denials, policy decisions, governance conflicts, and institutional precedence.

P11.10 may reference canonical authority/governance outputs as explicit evidence if a later slice defines that boundary, but it does not acquire authority to override them.

## AETHER-AIR relationship

P11.9 remains the canonical passive behavioral-intent and traceability layer.

P11.10 does not disguise advanced conditions, convergence, ranking, or Paradox Elevation as AETHER-AIR normalization, transformation, validation, projection, or reporting.

If later P11.10 slices consume AETHER-AIR, they must preserve the exact frozen P11.9 object and validation boundaries and produce distinct P11.10-owned semantic products.

## Determinism and provenance

Equivalent explicit canonical P11.10 inputs under the same explicit policy must produce equivalent P11.10 semantic products.

All condition evidence, candidate provenance, convergence evidence, selection/ranking evidence, and Paradox Elevation evidence must remain traceable to canonical predecessor inputs.

Deterministic ordering is not semantic precedence unless an explicit P11.10 policy says that a specific order field is operative.

## Non-execution boundary

P11.10 semantic analysis does not, merely by existing:

- execute AIR actions or pure functions;
- execute narrative choice paths or consequences;
- mutate runtime state;
- emit effects;
- grant or deny authority;
- modify governance state;
- load implementation providers;
- lower to Optimized AIR;
- generate Native Backend code;
- compile or execute backend artifacts;
- serialize itself into an implicit canonical wire/storage format;
- add CLI/editor/tooling authority.

Any later integration must be explicitly owned and separately tested.

## No preexisting Paradox Elevation implementation

The predecessor repository contains roadmap reservations and exclusion checks for Paradox Elevation, but no reviewed production Paradox Elevation semantic implementation.

P11.10 therefore begins by defining that semantic boundary rather than treating an older implementation as canonical.

## Recommended P11.10 decomposition

- **P11.10A:** architecture, predecessor compatibility, and ownership audit.
- **P11.10B:** minimal immutable advanced-condition, candidate-alternative, and outcome taxonomy.
- **P11.10C:** canonical condition evidence, admissibility, and deterministic higher-order evaluation contracts.
- **P11.10D:** semantic convergence-set construction and explicit convergence-policy boundary.
- **P11.10E:** deterministic convergence selection/composition/ranking under explicit P11.10-owned policy.
- **P11.10F:** Paradox Elevation eligibility and immutable elevated semantic-state contracts.
- **P11.10G:** validation, collision, closure, provenance, and extension contracts.
- **P11.10H:** downstream compatibility, reporting, tooling visibility, and traceability.
- **P11.10I:** final P11.10 integration regression and freeze.

This decomposition does not alter the macro-roadmap.

## P11.10A acceptance

P11.10A is accepted only when:

1. the branch descends exactly from the frozen P11.9I release;
2. frozen P11.9 AETHER-AIR production remains unchanged;
3. existing AIR/language/runtime/narrative conditional owners remain unchanged;
4. frozen Quad-Vector synchronization and resultant-resolution owners remain unchanged;
5. no predecessor production implementation already owns Paradox Elevation semantics;
6. the contract distinguishes advanced semantic conditionals from ordinary executable branches;
7. semantic convergence is explicitly distinct from Quad-Vector resultant resolution;
8. convergence precedence cannot be inferred from passive predecessor metadata;
9. Paradox Elevation preserves irreducible compatible evidence without silently selecting, averaging, or executing alternatives;
10. authority, governance, runtime, backend, serialization, CLI, editor, and tooling ownership remain unchanged;
11. the frozen P11.9I integration audit remains green in an isolated canonical checkout;
12. the P11.10A audit itself leaves repository state unchanged.

## Next stage

After P11.10A freezes, P11.10B may introduce the minimal immutable P11.10 semantic model and taxonomy.

P11.10B must remain data-first. Evaluation, convergence, ranking, Paradox Elevation eligibility, validation, reporting, and downstream integration remain owned by their later slices.
