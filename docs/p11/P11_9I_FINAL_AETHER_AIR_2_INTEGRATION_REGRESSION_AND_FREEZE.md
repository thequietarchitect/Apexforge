# P11.9I - Final AETHER-AIR 2.0 Integration Regression and Freeze

## Status

Final audit-only integration regression and freeze decision for the complete P11.9 AETHER-AIR 2.0 stack layered over the frozen P11.8H Parametric Semantic Lattice predecessor.

## Purpose

P11.9I performs the final observational integration audit for P11.9. It introduces no new AETHER-AIR semantic behavior and modifies no AETHER-AIR production module.

The stage verifies that P11.9A through P11.9H remain mutually compatible, that their annotated freeze chain is intact, that the complete AETHER-AIR public surface remains exactly the reviewed surface, that the complete B-through-H object lineage functions deterministically, and that the frozen P11.8H predecessor remains compatible.

If this audit passes, the complete P11.9 AETHER-AIR 2.0 stack is eligible for final freeze and publication.
