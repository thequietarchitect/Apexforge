## Changes and remaining scope

and `native_backend/native_execution.py`. New census, contract, native tests,
and RCV-52–55 documentation/evidence accompany them. Frozen grammar, compiler,
AIR, runtime, CLI, lifecycle ABI, historical test assertions, and tag objects
are unchanged. New acceptance tests intentionally do not join the frozen
`*_smoke_test.py` inventories; their commands are explicit above and in RCV-54.

Generated native test executables are temporary and removed by the tests.
Managed test bin/obj products are untracked. No generated binary was staged.
Artifact issuance is process-local; receipts are provenance checks rather
than remote signatures. Other AIR constructs continue to fail closed before
compiler discovery. Persistent artifact loading, other platforms, wider AIR
coverage, and broader behavioral equivalence remain outside this profile.
